Guided checklists (scenario packets)

A packet bundles the right documents, uploads, and readings into one ordered checklist for a client's situation. Assign one from here or from the client's record — it appears at the top of their portal home. Packet progress below is live (a step counts when the form is sent, the upload is accepted, or the reading is marked read). Every packet a client sees carries the line: “This checklist is guidance from our office, not legal advice about your situation.”
