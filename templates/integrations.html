How syncing works

  • Email: every 15 minutes the app pulls recent messages. They're matched to cases by the client's email address or a case number (like 2026-001) in the subject. Anything unmatched can be linked by hand from the case's Emails tab.
  • Calendar: upcoming deadlines and hearings are pushed to Google Calendar with the case number in the title. With import on, Google events come back into the app calendar.
  • Sending: the "Send email" button on a case sends through the connected account and logs the message to that case automatically.
